# Evidence, statistics and intellectual provenance

## Evidence levels

- `reference_verified`: **individual page URL** confirmed from public search. It does not imply its entire current content was crawled, every quote was checked, or the agent's explanation exactly reproduces unique page criteria.
- `family_level`: index title present and original family-level explanation/diagram prepared; **no independently verified deep link**.
- `requires_source_review`: author-specific pattern needs unique definition; do not invent a rule or purported example.
- `context_only`: ancillary index section, no executable price-geometry signal.

Do not conflate name-index coverage, correctness of each detailed rule, number of vector drawings, executable detector coverage, and independently validated strategy performance.

## Verify a reported win rate

1. Check whether a source means break-even failure, percent reaching target, percentage of winning trades, or something else.
2. Specify the bull/bear market, timeframe, breakout direction, price series and date when published.
3. Check sample size, filtering, look-ahead selection, entry price, rounding, overlapping trades, survivorship and regime dependence.
4. Financial performance requires out-of-sample after fees, slippage and liquidity. Backtested profitability is not a guarantee.
5. Show source link next to every sourced number. Do not treat synthetic diagrams as market observations.
